## 从“乘积到底要几个words”开始

先预测：两个8-bit unsigned values 213与197相乘得到41961，需要16 bits。若函数只返回low byte，结果是233；若要判断overflow或做fixed-point scaling，high byte 163同样关键。Multiplication的第一条contract不是algorithm，而是返回low word、high word还是full double word。

<Term def="Multiplier某个1-bit选择的一份shifted multiplicand，是schoolbook binary product的一个row。">部分积</Term>把乘法写成

$$
x\cdot y
=
\sum_{i=0}^{w-1}y_i(x2^i).
$$

<HD8ShiftAddLab />

<Term def="明确乘法结果保留low w bits、high w bits或完整2w bits的接口约定。">乘积宽度</Term>决定后续每个carry是否被保留。Low-word multiplication在unsigned modulo $2^w$与two's-complement signed bits上相同；high word则依赖unsigned或signed interpretation。

## 8.1 Binary multiplication and partial products

每个multiplier bit gate一个shifted row，所有rows按columns相加。Bit matrix中的carry向higher columns传播，和ordinary decimal long multiplication完全同构。

<HD8PartialProductLab />

Shift-add reference可逐bit处理multiplier，也可一次跳过zero runs。Hardware multiplier使用carry-save trees、Booth recoding或target-specific structures；book-level identity用于解释result，不代表software loop能胜过MUL instruction。

若只保留low $w$ bits，则每个partial product越过position $w-1$的部分都可discard。Unsigned与signed operands的low word一致，因为两种interpretations对modulo $2^w$ product同余：

$$
S_w(x)S_w(y)
\equiv
U_w(x)U_w(y)
\pmod{2^w}.
$$

## 8.2 Multiword multiplication

### Split operands into radix limbs

<Term def="以B等于2的b次方为base存储multiword integer的b-bit digit。">基数肢</Term>（limb）使mask和shift替代division。令$B=2^b$，

$$
X=x_1B+x_0,
\qquad
Y=y_1B+y_0.
$$

则

$$
XY
=
x_0y_0
+(x_0y_1+x_1y_0)B
+x_1y_1B^2.
$$

<Term def="把operands拆成limbs，计算所有pairwise partial products并按radix columns传播carry。">多字乘法</Term>（multiword multiplication）本质是polynomial convolution加base-$B$ normalization。

<HD8MultiwordLab />

### Carries belong to columns

Low product $x_0y_0$本身可能占2 limbs；两个cross products也各占2 limbs。Middle column必须加入low product high limb、两个cross low limbs，再把自己的carry传给high column。

<HD8CarryColumnsLab />

下面用16-bit halves构造32×32→64 unsigned product：

```c
typedef struct {
    uint32_t high;
    uint32_t low;
} u64_words;

static u64_words multiply_u32(uint32_t x, uint32_t y) {
    const uint32_t mask = UINT32_C(0xFFFF);
    uint32_t x0 = x & mask, x1 = x >> 16;
    uint32_t y0 = y & mask, y1 = y >> 16;
    uint32_t p00 = x0 * y0;
    uint32_t p01 = x0 * y1;
    uint32_t p10 = x1 * y0;
    uint32_t p11 = x1 * y1;

    uint32_t middle = (p00 >> 16) + (p01 & mask) + (p10 & mask);
    u64_words out;
    out.low = (p00 & mask) | (middle << 16);
    out.high = p11 + (p01 >> 16) + (p10 >> 16) + (middle >> 16);
    return out;
}
```

Each 16×16 product fits32 bits；middle maximum也fits32 bits。若limb更大而partial product不fit native type，需要更小limbs、special multiply-high primitive或carry-safe decomposition。

General $n$-limb multiplication computes $n^2$ limb products。Karatsuba等algorithm减少asymptotic multiplication count，但本章聚焦small fixed word counts的correct carry accounting。

## 8.3 High-order half of product

### Unsigned high product

<Term def="Full 2w-bit product中positions w到2w减1组成的word，等于unsigned exact product除以2的w次方取floor。">乘积高半部</Term>（high-order half of product）对unsigned operands定义

$$
\operatorname{mulhu}_w(x,y)
=
\left\lfloor\frac{U_w(x)U_w(y)}{2^w}\right\rfloor.
$$

Low half为product modulo $2^w$。

<HD8HighHalfLab />

High half用于detect unsigned overflow、fixed-point scaling、wide division与hash reduction。若只需要high，仍必须正确汇总cross terms与middle carry；可以不materializefull product，但不能忽略low columns产生的carry。

<HD8HighWithoutFullLab />

在上节half-limb construction中，`out.high`正是mulhu。Native ISA若有multiply-high，应优先让compiler/intrinsic表达；portable fallback可用half limbs并与double-width oracle比较。

Unsigned one-word product fits当且仅当high half为zero。若没有high primitive，也可在$y$nonzero时检查$x$是否不大于word maximum除以$y$，但division可能比wide multiply昂贵。

## 8.4 Signed and unsigned high-order product

### Same low half, different high half

8-bit pattern `11010011`可解释为unsigned 211或signed negative 45。与另一pattern相乘时low byte同余，但full signed product需要sign extension。

<HD8SignedProductLab />

令$x_u=U_w(x)$、$s_x$为sign bit的0/1 value，则

$$
x_s=x_u-s_x2^w,
\qquad
y_s=y_u-s_y2^w.
$$

展开signed product：

$$
x_sy_s
=
x_uy_u
-s_xy_u2^w
-s_yx_u2^w
+s_xs_y2^{2w}.
$$

最后一项在$2w$-bit product modulo $2^{2w}$中为zero，因此signed high word由unsigned high word减两个conditional corrections：

$$
\operatorname{mulhs}_w(x,y)
\equiv
\operatorname{mulhu}_w(x_u,y_u)
-s_xy_u
-s_yx_u
\pmod{2^w}.
$$

<Term def="把unsigned high product转换成signed high product时，对每个negative operand减去opposite unsigned operand的调整。">有符号高位修正</Term>。

<HD8SignedCorrectionLab />

```c
static uint32_t signed_high_from_unsigned(uint32_t x, uint32_t y) {
    u64_words product = multiply_u32(x, y);
    uint32_t high = product.high;
    if ((x >> 31) != 0u) high -= y;
    if ((y >> 31) != 0u) high -= x;
    return high;
}
```

Return bits interpreted assigned32 high half。The subtraction itself uses unsigned modulo arithmetic，avoiding signed overflow。Exhaustive 8-bit tests can compare all65536 input pairs against a16-bit signed oracle.

### Overflow depends on requested interpretation

Unsigned low-word overflow iff unsigned high word nonzero。Signed product fits one signed word ifffull 2w-bit product high half isexact sign extensionof low word's sign bit.

<HD8OverflowLab />

例如negative times negative可产生positive overflow，即使unsigned high nonzero criterion也为true但meaning不同。API必须说明checked signed、checked unsigned、modulo low word还是full result。

## 8.5 Multiplication by constants

### Binary expansion and addition chains

<Term def="从x开始，通过previous values的addition、subtraction和shifts构造constant times x的dependency graph。">加法链</Term>不只数set bits，还考虑共享intermediates与critical path。

Binary expansion为每个constant one bit生成shifted $x$：

<HD8ConstantBinaryLab />

Constant 45可写成32加8加4加1，也可共享intermediate，例如先构造$3x$、再$15x$、再$45x$。最少operators与最短latency不总一致；target可能把constant multiply直接作为single instruction。

接近power of two的constant常用subtract：

<HD8ConstantShortcutLab />

例如$15x=16x-x$。若signed source overflow undefined，compiler transformation仍需遵守language semantics；用unsigned fixed-width algebra时shift/add/sub按modulo更容易证明。

### Signed digits and NAF

<Term def="Digits取negative one、zero、positive one且nonzero digits不相邻的constant representation，常减少partial products。">非相邻形式</Term>（NAF）允许subtracted partial products。Runs of binary ones可改写为higher power minus lower power，例如

$$
00111100_2
=
01000000_2-00000100_2.
$$

<HD8NafLab />

NAF在non-adjacent constraint下具有minimal nonzero digit count，但hardware/compiler cost还取决于negate、fused address operations、register reuse与instruction scheduling。Production source通常写ordinary `x * constant`最能让optimizer工作；manual sequence需要generated-code evidence。

### Widen before multiplication

<Term def="Exact product超出requested result type range，导致wrap、trap、undefined behavior或checked failure。">乘法溢出</Term>要在operation之前防止。把already-narrow product cast到wider type不能恢复discarded high bits。

<HD8WideningLab />

```c
static uint64_t multiply_u32_wide(uint32_t x, uint32_t y) {
    return (uint64_t)x * (uint64_t)y;
}

static bool multiply_u32_checked(uint32_t x, uint32_t y, uint32_t *out) {
    uint64_t product = (uint64_t)x * (uint64_t)y;
    if (product > UINT32_MAX) return false;
    *out = (uint32_t)product;
    return true;
}
```

C expression cast必须作用于operand。For signed values，use a wider signed type only when it truly covers full product range，or use checked builtins/library APIs.

<Callout type="trap" title="Signed和unsigned乘法low bits相同，所以high bits也相同">
Low half只看modulo 2的w次方；signed operands代表xu减sign乘2的w次方，cross terms正好改变high word。修法是使用两个conditional sign corrections或native signed multiply-high。
</Callout>

<Callout type="trap" title="乘完再cast就得到了wide product">
Narrow multiplication已先wrap或触发signed undefined behavior，later cast只扩展错误的low result。修法是在operator执行前cast至少一个operand到足以容纳full product的type。
</Callout>

## 小结

Chapter 8 Multiplication从partial products出发。Multiword multiplication把operands拆成radix limbs，low product、cross products与high product按columns相加，middle carry决定full result。

High-order half of product是unsigned exact product除以$2^w$的floor。Signed and unsigned high-order product共享low half，却相差由两个sign bits控制的opposite-operand corrections。

Multiplication by constants可用binary shifts、addition chains或NAF signed digits减少partial products，但modern compiler/ISA可能已有更好sequence。所有实现都必须先固定product width、signedness与overflow policy，并在乘法之前widen。

<Exercises>

**问题 1**：为什么16-bit operands拆成8-bit limbs后会有四个pairwise partial products？

<Answer>
每个operand有low和high两个base-256 digits。Distributive expansion产生x0y0、x0y1、x1y0、x1y1四对；cross pair权重为B，high pair权重为B平方，随后按columns传播carry。
</Answer>

**问题 2**：Unsigned 8-bit 213乘197的high与low halves分别是多少？

<Answer>
Exact product为41961。除以256的quotient为163、remainder为233，所以high byte是163即10100011，low byte是233即11101001。
</Answer>

**问题 3**：把unsigned high product转signed high时，为何negative x要减unsigned y？

<Answer>
Signed x等于unsigned x减2的w次方。乘y后多出negative y乘2的w次方，正好在high word中减y；negative y同理减x。双negative的2的2w次方项在2w-bit product中消失。
</Answer>

**问题 4**：Constant 15为何可用一次shift和一次subtract，且这不自动证明比MUL更快？

<Answer>
15x等于16x减x，所以source sequence可写成x left-shift 4再减x。Target MUL可能是一条高throughput instruction，compiler也可能自己选择该sequence；latency、ports和surrounding dependencies仍需查看generated code和benchmark。
</Answer>
