## 从“除数已知”到底提供了什么开始

先预测：negative 37除8，toward-zero quotient为negative 4，而arithmetic right shift 3通常给negative 5。Constant divisor让我们预先计算mask、fixed-point reciprocal、modular inverse或shift-subtract schedule，但它不会消除rounding与overflow semantics。

<Term def="对negative dividend在right shift前添加2的k次方减1，使floor shift变为toward-zero quotient的conditional adjustment。">负数偏置</Term>、<Term def="近似2的p次方除以constant divisor的integer，用multiply-high和shift替代runtime division。">魔数乘子</Term>与<Term def="在modulo 2的w次方ring中与odd divisor相乘得到1的word。">模逆</Term>服务不同contracts。Magic reciprocal求ordinary floor/trunc quotient；modular inverse只在known-exact division中恢复quotient。混用两者是本章最危险的错误。

## 10.1 Signed division by a known power of 2

### Arithmetic shift rounds the wrong way for negative nonmultiples

设$d=2^k$。Unsigned quotient是logical right shift。Signed arithmetic right shift通常等于floor：

$$
n\mathbin{\mathrm{ashr}}k
=
\left\lfloor\frac{n}{2^k}\right\rfloor.
$$

若language signed division向zero截断，negative且not exactly divisible时floor少1。令sign mask $s=n\mathbin{\mathrm{ashr}}(w-1)$，bias为$s\mathbin{\&}(2^k-1)$：

$$
q
=
(n+(s\mathbin{\&}(2^k-1)))
\mathbin{\mathrm{ashr}}k.
$$

Nonnegative $s=0$无bias；negative $s$为all ones，bias是$d-1$。

<HD10SignedPowerLab />

```c
static int32_t divide_power2_toward_zero(int32_t n, unsigned k) {
    // Precondition: k is in [0, 30].
    uint32_t mask = (UINT32_C(1) << k) - 1u;
    uint32_t sign = (uint32_t)(n >> 31);
    int32_t bias = (int32_t)(sign & mask);
    return (n + bias) >> k;
}
```

当$k=0$，mask和bias为zero。Shift count不能等于width。Bias addition不会overflow：negative $n$至多加$2^k-1$，仍不超过signed maximum。

### Remainder follows the chosen quotient convention

Toward-zero remainder由

$$
r=n-q2^k
$$

恢复，满足$|r|\lt2^k$且nonzero remainder与$n$同号。

<HD10PowerRemainderLab />

用`n & (d - 1)`得到的是nonnegative modulo remainder，适合unsigned或Euclidean convention，不等于negative dividend的toward-zero remainder。Quotient和remainder必须来自同一rounding contract。

## 10.2 Signed division and remainder by non-powers of 2

### Fixed-point reciprocal

<Term def="把1除constant d放大2的p次方并round为integer，用product high bits近似n除d的representation。">定点倒数</Term>选择precision $p$和integer $M$，使

$$
\frac{M}{2^p}
\approx
\frac1d.
$$

Runtime计算$Mn$的high bits并right shift。Generator必须证明approximation error对整个input range不会把result推过integer quotient boundary；signed negative inputs还需rounding correction。

8-bit signed divide by3的一个完整例子：

$$
M=86=\left\lceil\frac{2^8}{3}\right\rceil,
\qquad
q=\operatorname{mulhs}_8(n,86)
-(n\mathbin{\mathrm{ashr}}7).
$$

Negative sign mask为negative one，subtract它等于加1，把multiply-high的floor改为toward-zero。

<HD10SignedMagicLab />

```c
static int8_t divide_i8_by_3(int8_t n) {
    int16_t product = (int16_t)n * 86;
    int16_t high = product >> 8;
    int16_t sign = n >> 7;
    return (int8_t)(high - sign);
}
```

Width、divisor、rounding和magic constant是一组不可拆的parameters。把86复制到16/32-bit code不会成立。

<HD10SignedMagicProofLab />

Small-width exhaustive testing可覆盖全部256 signed inputs；proof仍要说明为什么error bound成立，而不是把passing table当作all-width theorem。

### Remainder from the proven quotient

一旦quotient已证明正确，

$$
r=n-qd
$$

给出matching remainder。Constant $d$ multiplication通常又能由shift-add/sub优化。

<HD10RemainderFromQuotientLab />

不要另写一套independent remainder magic，除非它有独立价值；共享quotient使$n=qd+r$成为强cross-check。

## 10.3 Unsigned division

Unsigned没有negative rounding correction，却仍要选足够precision。8-bit divide by3可使用

$$
M=171
=
\left\lceil\frac{2^9}{3}\right\rceil,
\qquad
q=\left\lfloor\frac{171n}{2^9}\right\rfloor.
$$

<HD10UnsignedMagicLab />

这个formula对$n$从0到255可穷举验证。General generator对divisor $d$选择post-shift $s$与$M$，目标形式常是

$$
q
=
\left\lfloor
\frac{Mn}{2^{w+s}}
\right\rfloor.
$$

某些divisors可用simple multiply-high plus shift；另一些需要<Term def="当simple reciprocal product略低时，把n或由n减high product得到的fraction加入中间值的generator flag。">加法标记</Term>（add indicator）或不同precision。Production实现应由trusted generator/compiler产生，而非手抄邻近divisor的magic。

Unsigned remainder仍用$n-qd$。若只需remainder，digit folding或special modulus algorithm可能更便宜。

## 10.4 Exact division by constants

### Odd divisors have inverses modulo powers of two

<Term def="Caller已经证明n可被d整除，目标是恢复exact quotient而不处理floor或remainder。">常数精确除法</Term>（exact division by constants）允许更强precondition。若odd $d$有inverse $d^{-1}$满足

$$
dd^{-1}\equiv1\pmod{2^w},
$$

且$n=dq$、$q$可在word中表示，则

$$
q\equiv nd^{-1}\pmod{2^w}.
$$

8-bit下3的inverse为171，因为$3\cdot171=513\equiv1\pmod{256}$。

<HD10ExactInverseLab />

```c
static uint8_t exact_divide_u8_by_3(uint8_t n) {
    // Precondition: n % 3 == 0.
    return (uint8_t)(n * UINT8_C(171));
}
```

若precondition不成立，result只是modular product，与floor quotient无关。Example $n=1$得到171，不是zero。

<HD10InverseExplorerLab />

Even divisor没有modulo-$2^w$ inverse。Factor $d=2^k u$且$u$odd；known exact divisibility保证$n$low $k$ bits为zero，先right shift $k$，再乘$u$的inverse。Shift direction与signedness仍需定义。

## 10.5 Remainder by summing digits

设base-$B$ expansion

$$
n=\sum_{i=0}^{t}a_iB^i.
$$

若$B\equiv1\pmod d$，则

$$
n\bmod d
=
\left(\sum_i a_i\right)\bmod d.
$$

<Term def="利用radix B在modulo d下等于正一或负一，把wide number反复折叠成digit sum或alternating sum。">数位折叠</Term>（remainder by summing digits）可多轮执行直到sum很小。

因为$4\equiv1\pmod3$，binary word按2-bit base-4 digits分组后，其sum modulo3等于original remainder。

<HD10DigitSumLab />

若$B\equiv-1\pmod d$，powers交替正负，使用alternating digit sum。Binary base2对modulo3就是negative one：

<HD10AlternatingDigitsLab />

Sum可能仍大于$d$，需要再次fold、conditional subtract或small table normalization。Signed negative inputs先映射到明确modulo convention。

## 10.6 Methods not using multiply high

有些targets没有multiply-high，或latency不合适。Alternatives包括：

- Pre-shifted divisor的fixed-width compare/subtract schedule。
- Reciprocal approximation用ordinary low multiply加add/shift sequence，并通过error correction。
- Remainder digit folding，若只需要modulus。
- Small bounded domain lookup table。
- Bit-at-a-time long division作为constant-time-ish reference。

<HD10NoMulHighLab />

```c
static div_u32_result divide_u8_by_7_no_multiply(uint8_t n) {
    div_u32_result out = {0u, n};
    for (int bit = 7; bit >= 0; --bit) {
        uint32_t shifted = UINT32_C(7) << bit;
        if (shifted <= out.remainder) {
            out.remainder -= shifted;
            out.quotient |= UINT32_C(1) << bit;
        }
    }
    return out;
}
```

Loop在这个width固定执行8 steps，but compiler may unroll and remove impossible high shifts。它是correct fallback，不一定是fastest。Ordinary source `n / constant`通常最利于compiler选择target-specific magic sequence；manual code需要generated instruction comparison。

## 小结

Chapter 10 Integer Division by Constants首先处理signed division by a known power of 2：negative bias把arithmetic-shift floor改为toward-zero，remainder从$n-q2^k$恢复。

Signed division and remainder by non-powers of 2与unsigned division使用width-specific fixed-point reciprocal、multiply-high、shift和可能的add indicator。Magic constants必须由error proof和full-domain oracle共同验证。

Exact division by constants使用odd divisor modular inverse，前提是known divisibility；even divisor先移除power-of-two factor。Remainder by summing digits利用base在modulo divisor下等于positive/negative one。Methods not using multiply high则提供shift-subtract、digit folding与table fallbacks。

<Exercises>

**问题 1**：为什么negative 37 arithmetic-shift right 3得到negative 5，而toward-zero division应为negative 4？Bias是多少？

<Answer>
Arithmetic shift对应floor，negative 37除8的floor是negative 5；toward-zero为negative 4。Negative bias取8减1等于7，negative 37加7为negative 30，再arithmetic shift 3得到negative 4。
</Answer>

**问题 2**：8-bit signed divide by3为何在multiply-high 86之后还要subtract sign mask？

<Answer>
Multiply-high对negative product执行floor，可能比toward-zero quotient小1。Sign mask对negative为negative one，subtract它等于加1；nonnegative sign mask为zero，不改变result。
</Answer>

**问题 3**：为什么3的8-bit modular inverse 171不能用于计算1除3的floor？

<Answer>
Inverse identity只保证3乘171在modulo256下为1。Exact division要求n等于3q；n为1不满足，1乘171得到171只是modular residue，不是floor quotient zero。
</Answer>

**问题 4**：为什么把8-bit number按2-bit base-4 digits求和能保留modulo3 remainder？

<Answer>
Four modulo3等于1，所以每个4的i次方也等于1。Number的每项ai乘4的i次方在modulo3下都等于ai，原数与digit sum同余，再对sum取modulo3即可。
</Answer>
