## 从“matching都叫匹配，目标却完全不同”开始

先预测：Cardinality matching要尽量多pair；stable marriage要求没有双方都想背离当前partners的pair；assignment要在perfect matching中maximize total weight。三者都在bipartite graph上选不共享endpoints的edges，但“最优”证书不可互换。

是共同结构。第24章用unit flow求maximum cardinality；本章重新审视更快的augmenting-path phases，并增加preference与weighted perfect matching两类目标。


## 25.1 Maximum bipartite matching (revisited)

### Alternating and augmenting paths

 $P$满足：

$$
M'=M\triangle E(P),
\qquad
|M'|=|M|+1.
$$


Berge theorem：Matching $M$为maximum当且仅当不存在$M$-augmenting path。若有path显然可增长；若另一matching $M^*$更大，考虑$M\triangle M^*$的components，它们是alternating cycles和paths，必有一个component包含比$M$多一条$M^*$ edge，即$M$ augmenting path。


逐次DFS找任意augmenting path最多做$O(V)$次，每次$O(E)$，为$O(VE)$。Hopcroft-Karp通过一phase同时处理多个shortest augmenting paths。

### Hopcroft-Karp phases

从所有free left vertices同时BFS。Unmatched edges从left走向right，matched edge从right回到left；首次到达free right的layer决定当前shortest augmenting length。


```text
HOPCROFT-KARP(G)
1  M = empty matching
2  while BFS builds layers reaching a free right vertex
3      for each free left vertex u
4          DFS within layers to find vertex-disjoint shortest augmenting paths
5      augment M along all paths found in this phase
6  return M
```


每phase的BFS与全部DFS总$O(E)$。同时消除最大组shortest paths后，下一phase shortest augmenting length严格增加；short paths phases和long paths剩余数量的组合论证给$O(\sqrt V)$ phases：

$$
T(V,E)=O(E\sqrt V).
$$

DFS失败时可把vertex标为本phase dead，避免重复扫描。Parallel augmentation要求paths vertex-disjoint；若共享matched vertex，同时flip会破坏matching。


## 25.2 The stable-marriage problem

### Preferences and blocking pairs

不优化total rank。输入是完整strict preference lists；ties、incomplete lists与capacities属于扩展模型，需要新的stability定义或算法细节。


 $(m,w)$满足$m$偏好$w$胜过$M(m)$，且$w$偏好$m$胜过$M(w)$。


### Deferred acceptance

Proposer-oriented Gale-Shapley算法让free proposer向尚未proposal的最高rank receiver申请。Receiver在当前hold和new proposal中保留更偏好的一个，reject另一个；held不是final，直到所有proposers matched。

```text
DEFERRED-ACCEPTANCE
1  while some proposer m is free and has an untried receiver
2      w = highest-ranked receiver not yet proposed to by m
3      if w is free, w tentatively holds m
4      else if w prefers m to current m'
5          w rejects m' and holds m
6      else w rejects m
7  return held pairs
```


每ordered pair最多proposal一次，时间$O(n^2)$。Receiver held partner只会变好，proposer每次rejection后选择只会变差，算法终止且在complete balanced lists上得到perfect matching。

稳定性反证：若$(m,w)$是blocking pair，$m$必在向current partner前曾向更喜欢的$w$ proposal。$w$当时reject $m$或以后换成更喜欢者，所以final partner不可能比$m$差，矛盾。


更强结论：所有proposers都获得其在任何stable matching中可得到的best partner，receivers则得到worst stable partner。交换proposal side会选择stable-matching lattice的另一端。


## 25.3 The Hungarian algorithm for the assignment problem

### Maximum-weight perfect matching

用$n\times n$ weight matrix表示。目标：

$$
\max_{M\text{ perfect}}\sum_{(x,y)\in M}w(x,y).
$$


### Feasible labels and equality graph

使用labels $l(v)$满足：

$$
l(x)+l(y)\ge w(x,y)
\quad\text{for all }(x,y)\in E.
$$

初始可令left label为该row maximum、right label为0。Equality graph $E_l$含所有tight edges：

$$
E_l=\{(x,y):l(x)+l(y)=w(x,y)\}.
$$


对任意perfect matching $M$：

$$
w(M)\le\sum_{v\in X\cup Y}l(v).
$$

若equality graph中存在perfect matching，全部matched edges tight，weight恰等于label sum upper bound，因而optimal。


### Creating new equality edges

若equality graph当前matching没有augmenting path，从free left root建立alternating tree，令$S$为reached left vertices，$T$为reached right vertices。取：

$$
\delta=
\min_{\substack{x\in S\\y\notin T}}
\{l(x)+l(y)-w(x,y)\}.
$$

把$S$ labels减$\delta$，$T$ labels加$\delta$，其他不变。$S$到$T$ tight edges仍tight，所有constraints保持feasible，且至少一条$S$到$Y-T$ edge变tight，equality graph扩展。


```text
HUNGARIAN-MAX-WEIGHT
1  initialize feasible labels and an equality-graph matching
2  while matching is not perfect
3      grow an alternating tree in the equality graph
4      if a free right vertex is reached, augment
5      else compute minimum slack delta
6           decrease labels on S; increase labels on T
7           continue with newly tight edges
8  return the perfect equality matching and labels
```

Dense implementation为$O(n^3)$。Min-cost版本可negate weights或使用dual potentials，但必须一致调整inequality direction与numeric overflow。


## 小结

Chapter 25 Matchings in Bipartite Graphs在maximum bipartite matching revisited中用Berge theorem与Hopcroft-Karp把复杂度降到$O(E\sqrt V)$。Stable-marriage problem用deferred acceptance产生无blocking pair的matching，并揭示proposer-optimal偏向。Hungarian algorithm for the assignment problem维护feasible labels、equality graph和slack，得到$O(n^3)$ maximum-weight perfect matching。

三种matching共享“不重复endpoints”，但证书完全不同。Maximum cardinality交付无augmenting path，stability交付无blocking pair，assignment交付feasible dual labels与perfect tight matching；只报告pairs无法证明对应目标。
